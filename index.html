SkinDeep

Research notes and experiments by Steve Seguin.

SkinDeep started with a simple idea: show people generated images, learn which ones they like, then use that feedback to suggest new ones.

The work now includes small models that return a decision or a click location, and experiments that test whether a model can stop processing early.
