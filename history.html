Original videos, code and documents

2018–19 · Preference learning

SkinDeep explored personal preference models trained from ratings of generated images. The archived mobile app and engine, original videos, and 2019 provisional filing document the work. Descriptions in a filing are not proof that every proposed application shipped.

2025 · PLGL write-up

The method was written up under the name Preference Learning in Generative Latent Spaces. Some broad claims and proposed integrations in those documents require qualification. The historical text remains available beside current corrections.

2026 · A broader lab

The site now separates preference learning, direct decisions, spatial outputs, and adaptive computation. The continuing-research register keeps pilots distinct from open questions.
