About SkinDeep

A research project by Steve Seguin.

SkinDeep began in 2018 with an app that learned visual preferences from likes and dislikes on generated faces.

This site keeps that work available and documents newer experiments: returning labels, pointing at things on screen, and stopping a model before it runs every processing step.

The demos and test results are here so you can see what works and what still needs work.
