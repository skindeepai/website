These 30 PNG files are byte-for-byte copies of the preselected public research
sample in results/screenspot/data-manifest.json. No screenshot pixels were
altered. Coordinates and target-box overlays are rendered separately by the
demo. Original source image hashes are retained in gallery.json and the
original data manifest.

Depicted third-party applications, websites, artwork and marks retain their
respective rights. The dataset license is not a claim of ownership over that
underlying content. This subset is presented with the recorded model outputs
for inspection of the ScreenSpot localization experiment.
