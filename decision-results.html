Ways to make a decision sooner

The answer can be a label, such as SAFE or BLOCK. We tested several ways to get there.

Test results

1. Stop at a fixed pointUse the first quarter, half or three quarters of Qwen. Compare the mistakes at each depth.2. Check before continuingA small checker decides whether to return a label or run more layers. Different messages can stop at different points.3. Try a tiny model firstAccept its confident answers. Send the other messages to a larger model.4. Teach the model differentlyTrain intermediate answers, learn from another model, or remove later layers.

These are alternative approaches. A faster result only helps if the mistakes are acceptable.
