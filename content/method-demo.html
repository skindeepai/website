Models, downloads and limits

The main model has 11.1 million parameters and four layers. Its early classifier reads layer 2. The independent tiny model has 4.37 million parameters and two layers. A cascade fallback runs both models: two tiny blocks plus four larger blocks; it shares no hidden states.

The initial graphs total about 89 MB. Cascade adds 17.5 MB; updated training adds 44.5 MB; INT8 adds 35.2 MB. Tokenizers and runtime are additional. Downloads start after a button press.
