Test results · Decision methods

Dataset: ToxicChat0124 · 100 previously inspected messages: 50 toxic, 50 benign. A small exploratory test, not live moderation.

A small classifier reads Qwen’s internal numbers at the chosen layer and returns SAFE or BLOCK. It does not write a reply.

Layer 12 skipped half the blocks and took about half the time, with six fewer correct answers. Layer 18 corrected eight errors but introduced seven others; its slightly higher total is not lossless performance.
