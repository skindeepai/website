About this search

Answers are prepared from the research pages, with a link to each source. Search finds existing explanations; it does not write new answers. Open the source for full study setup, measurements and limits.

Keyword matching works immediately. Meaning-based matching runs on your device and loads about 50 MB of model and runtime files on the first search. Your question stays in this browser; no AI service receives it. If the model cannot load, keyword search stays available.
