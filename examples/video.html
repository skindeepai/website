Video edits and effects

Learn which cuts and visual styles work.

A creator could compare short edits, transitions or colour treatments, then use those choices to guide the next set of variations.

Where the small model fits

A video model represents candidate clips. A small scorer learns preferences across whole clips rather than judging only a still frame; generation and editing remain separate work.

What would need to work

Use unseen clips and measure continuity, viewer preference and full rendering cost. No video preference model is running in the drawing demo.
