Use case · Idea to test

News and content curation

Find useful articles in a preferred format.

A reader could prefer concise local reporting in the morning and detailed analysis later. The original proposal covered interest-based ranking and different ways of presenting headlines or stories.

Where the small model fits

A personal scorer ranks article representations. A separate writing model may change length or presentation while retaining the source facts and attribution; a preference score is not a fact check.

What would need to work

Measure usefulness, factual consistency and breadth of coverage. The system should not alter reported events to make a story more pleasing. No news-personalization study has been run here.
