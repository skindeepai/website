Fashion and outfit choices

Find clothes that fit your style.

A shopper could rate a few outfits, then see clothing or custom-design variations closer to their taste. Outfit recommendations, garment design and trend exploration were original proposals.

Where the small model fits

A visual model describes each item. A personal scorer ranks options within the chosen size, price and stock limits. A generator could also propose styles, separately from the catalog of things that can actually be bought.

What would need to work

Measure choices on unseen outfits and distinguish liking a picture from fit, comfort or purchase intent. A personal taste model does not establish population-wide fashion trends.
