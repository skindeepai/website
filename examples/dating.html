Use case · Idea to test

Mutual visual preferences

Look for interest from both people.

The original dating proposal used ratings of generated faces to learn visual preferences, then considered whether two consenting people might find each other appealing. Each person would still decide whether to connect.

Where the small model fits

Each person has a preference scorer. Representations of real photos could be scored in both directions, rather than relying on a single popularity rating. This requires an encoder compatible with the representation used for training.

What would need to work

Test mutual visual interest and privacy separately. Encoded photos and preference scores can reveal information; they are not automatically private. Visual appeal is not relationship compatibility, and the original face-generation demo was not a validated matching service.
