Idea to test

Mutual preferences

A match needs interest from both people.

One proposed use is to estimate whether two people are likely to find each other visually appealing. Both people would need to agree to take part.

Visual preference is not relationship compatibility. Encoded photos are not automatically private; that needs separate testing.

Proposed test and earlier examples

Proposed integration. No validated end-to-end mutual visual matching result is claimed here.

Experiment P11 / P14

Evaluate two-sided visual preference against one-sided and generic ranking. Define who can access embeddings and query scores before testing privacy.

Measure: Mutual visual preference, coverage, reconstruction/identification exposure, and utility lost to protection. This does not measure relationship compatibility.
