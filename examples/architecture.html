Architecture and interiors

Explore layouts and rooms that fit your taste.

A homeowner could compare room layouts, materials or facade designs while keeping the room size and budget fixed. Residential layouts, commercial facades and urban planning appeared in the original application list.

Where the small model fits

A design model generates or represents options. A preference scorer ranks them, while independent rules keep dimensions and other requirements fixed. The scorer can guide appearance; it does not certify a building.

What would need to work

Test whether people prefer the proposed options and whether every design still meets the specified constraints. Start with a room-layout task before making broader architecture claims.
