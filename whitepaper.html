Technical errata

  1. Inversion: a score usually maps to many latent inputs. Specify an optimization objective, bounds, and feasibility.
  2. Classifier: the browser demo uses logistic regression; SVMs and deeper feature-space networks are different alternatives.
  3. Performance: millisecond training is a scoped measurement, not end-to-end generation latency. Old multiplier claims lack attached reproducible runs.
  4. Multiple modes: randomized search does not give a linear classifier disconnected preference regions.
  5. Privacy and validity: embeddings and negative examples do not establish universal guarantees.
  6. Transfer: generators and domains need compatible representations or learned alignment.
