Decisions without a written reply

Measured pilot

A label in one step

For a yes/no, a choice or a label, switch thinking off and allow only the label words.

Test design and detailed evidence

80 easy items (yes/no, A-D choice, sentiment, routing). Qwen3.8-27B (FP8 weights, 16-bit cache) on two Intel Arc Pro B70 cards. One pass.

How the label is producedSame label as normal decodingRightMedian time per item
One step, labels only80 of 8078 of 800.09 s
Normal decoding, thinking off–78 of 800.09 s
With thinking (24 items)–23 of 240.59 s

Same answers, 6.5 times faster than thinking on easy items. The one-step label agreed with the thinking answer on 24 of 24. With drafting, a short label already takes one step, so the saving is the thinking. The restriction adds a guarantee that the answer is a valid label.

Not measured: hard decisions where thinking changes the answer.
